About the Summit

The Nashville Energy & Mining Summit is where power is treated as an asset — and where the people generating, moving, and monetizing it get in the same room.

NEMS27 is a curated gathering of approximately 200 operators, engineers, policymakers, entrepreneurs, capital allocators, and institutional leaders who are shaping the future of energy, grids, and compute. United by a shared mission, we are pioneering new models for how power is generated, distributed, and monetized in the age of Bitcoin and beyond.

No expo hall. No sales decks. Firesides, panels, and working conversations across two days at Bitcoin Park — the community-supported campus in Nashville that has hosted NEMS since 2024 — followed by the dinners and late nights where the real deals get done.

Programming Tracks

Generation to load, and everything in between

Six lenses on the same question: how does a megawatt become money — and who gets to decide?

01 — Generation

Powering the Future

Innovations in energy production, from natural gas and renewables to nuclear and SMRs. How diverse sources of generation can be harnessed, monetized, and integrated into a more resilient energy landscape that supports both local communities and global markets.

02 — Transmission

Building the Modern Grid

The evolving role of transmission operators, utilities, and engineers in optimizing grid dynamics. Grid flexibility, stability, and the new business models that emerge when power is treated not only as a utility but also as an asset.

03 — Load

Bitcoin, AI, and Beyond

Demand-side innovation: Bitcoin mining, AI, HPC, and other compute-intensive industries as flexible loads that stabilize grids, create economic opportunity, and open new pathways for monetizing the megawatt.

04 — Markets

Markets & Monetization

The economics of energy and compute: energy trading, capital allocation, treasury strategy, and the mechanisms by which megawatts become monetized — from local power purchase agreements to global-scale markets.

05 — Policy

Policy, Security & Governance

The governmental frameworks that shape the industry. Policymakers, regulators, legal experts, and security specialists on enabling policy, grid sovereignty, energy independence, and governance models that unlock domestic and international flourishing.

06 — Technology

The State of the Art

The technological innovations that let Bitcoin mining and AI superclusters happen all over the world — connecting technical expertise with the operators experimenting and proving out these technologies in the field.
